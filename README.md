# AGILITA AG: Enterprise Cloud Transformation Case Study

An enterprise business analysis and cloud architecture evaluation of AGILITA AG, completed as part of the **SAP Professional Fundamentals** course (SAP Technology Consultant Professional Certificate on **Coursera**).

---

## Project Summary
* **Organization:** AGILITA AG (SAP Gold Partner)
* **Program:** Coursera & SAP Technology Consultant Specialization
* **Focus:** Digital Transformation, Cloud ERP Migration, Systems Thinking
* **Core Solutions:** SAP S/4HANA Cloud, SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP), SAP Analytics Cloud

---

## Key Insights

* **Macro Trends & Drivers:** Accelerated SaaS adoption, demands for unified real-time analytics, and transitioning away from siloed legacy tools.
* **Business Motivations:** Streamline internal Professional Services workflows (resource planning, billing) and establish an agile reference architecture for clients.
* **Analysis Frameworks:** 
  * **Systems Thinking:** Mapped interconnected operations across talent management, project delivery, and billing loops.
  * **PESTLE Analysis:** Assessed market competition, data governance, and cloud adoption agility.

---

## Project Team Roles & Governance

* **Business Process Consultant (Selected Role):** Aligns business processes with SAP standard best practices across finance and consulting delivery.
* **Project Manager:** Oversees project baselines (scope, schedule, cost) and agile delivery sprints.
* **Solution Architect:** Governs system integration standards, clean-core architecture, and SAP BTP extensions.
* **Functional Consultant:** Configures module workflows and end-to-end business logic.

## Methodology & Framework
* **Framework:** SAP Activate (Discover, Prepare, Explore, Realize, Deploy, Run)
* **Core Competencies:** Business Process Mapping, Stakeholder Management, Enterprise Discovery
